🏛️ OpenGrantStack Governance AI Assistant

The Governance AI Assistant enforces governance rules, validates policy changes, and ensures compliance across all OpenGrantStack repositories.

This assistant ensures:

- Governance policy enforcement  
- Review of governance PRs  
- Funding decision validation  
- Template and structure compliance  
- Governance registry maintenance
